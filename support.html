Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Each case presents a letter grid with hidden words. Swipe or drag to select letters and find all listed words. Once you've found enough clues, fill in the killer, method, and location to solve the case. Tap "How to Play" from the main menu for a full tutorial.

How do hints and reveals work?

You can use free hints and reveals at the start of each case. After that, you may watch a short rewarded video ad to unlock additional hints or letter reveals. Watching ads is always optional.

Why do I see ads?

ClueSearch is free to play and supported by advertisements. Interstitial ads may appear after certain cases. Rewarded video ads are only shown when you choose to watch them for in-game rewards.

I lost my game progress. Can you restore it?

Game progress is saved locally on your device. If you cleared app data, switched devices, or reinstalled the App, progress cannot be recovered from our side. We recommend avoiding clearing app data unless necessary.

How do I turn off vibration?

Open the menu from the main screen and toggle the vibration setting off. You can also manage sound preferences from the same menu.

How do I rate the app?

You may be prompted to rate ClueSearch on Google Play after completing cases. You can also visit the app's page on Google Play and leave a review at any time.
