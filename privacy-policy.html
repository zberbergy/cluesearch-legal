Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ClueSearch ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the ClueSearch mobile application (the "App"), available on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

ClueSearch is designed to work with minimal data collection. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your game progress, including the current case and solved cases
  • In-progress puzzle state for each case
  • Your sound and vibration preferences

This information is saved on your device using local storage and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When you view or interact with ads, Google may collect and use information in accordance with its own privacy policies, which may include:

  • Device identifiers, such as advertising ID
  • IP address and general location (country/region level)
  • App usage and ad interaction data

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or limit ad personalization through your device settings (for example, by opting out of personalized ads in your Android privacy settings).

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — to display interstitial and rewarded video advertisements
  • Google Play In-App Review — to optionally prompt you to rate the App on Google Play

These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies. We do not control how third parties collect or use your information.

4. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Internet — required to load and display advertisements
  • Vibrate — used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay, if enabled in settings

5. Children's Privacy

ClueSearch is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App or its third-party services, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Data Security

Because game data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for keeping your device secure. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data will remove locally stored progress.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Us
